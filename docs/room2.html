About This Room

The Spring 2024 capstone project brings users through a series of puzzles including a Ceasar Cipher. Use the made artifacts and on-screen videos to complete the puzzles, discover passwords, important info, and complete the room!

How to Play / Set Up

  • Can be played with 1-4 players
  • Ensure the user has: a manilla folder of images, a manilla folder of artifacts, a brief case, and a lock box
  • The brief case contains scrap paper and a pen
  • Facilitator should have access to the red binder titled "SET UP & SOLUTIONS"
  • Begin by reading the paper titled "Introduction" in the introduction and song lyrics folder
