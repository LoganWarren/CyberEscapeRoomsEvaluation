Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q: Do I need special hardware?
    A: Most rooms can be played with a laptop and internet connection.
  • Q: How long does a game last?
    A: Typically 30–60 minutes depending on the group.

Troubleshoot Guide

If you encounter issues:

  1. Check your internet connection.
  2. Follow the setup instructions on each room’s page.
  3. If problems persist, see the GitHub repo to report issues.
