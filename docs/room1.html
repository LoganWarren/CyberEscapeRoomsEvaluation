About This Room

This 2022 Fall capstone delivers a classic cyber escape room: teams progress through themed puzzle groups (Phishing, OSINT, Botnet, Brute Force, Ransomware/QR/Stego, etc.). Use printed artifacts and on-screen clues to unlock passwords, decode hints, and complete the scenario.

How to Play / Set Up

  • Recommended: 1–4 players, latest Chrome/Firefox, speakers on.
  • Facilitator prints needed PDFs (see Puzzles & Materials below) and distributes to teams.
  • Teams may use the web “Website Code” version for interactive steps (optional).
